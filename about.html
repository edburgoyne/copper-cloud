WHY WE'RE HERE

Play is just the beginning.

We believe playing a game can be the first step toward asking, “How did someone make that?” Copper Cloud brings together browser games and the tools to help curious people find out.

Our name takes inspiration from the copperclouds in Brandon Sanderson's Mistborn stories: a little bit of mystery for a place full of imagination. Copper Cloud is an independent project.

Here, Python, Pygame, and AI-assisted development are ways to learn by making. An assistant can help explain and experiment, but the ideas, choices, and understanding belong to the creator.
